Option Buying Course

Learn to buy calls and puts with a clear understanding of premium, the Greeks and volatility — the foundation every options trader needs.

₹3,999 2 Days Beginner-friendlyOnsite & Online

The Option Buying course is where most traders begin. Over two practical days you will understand exactly what you are buying, why an option gains or loses value, and how time and volatility work for and against a buyer.

What you'll learn

1. Foundation (Basics)

  • What options are — calls and puts explained simply
  • Premium, strike price, spot price and expiry
  • ITM, ATM and OTM explained
  • Intrinsic value vs time value
  • Lot size and the margin a buyer needs

2. The Greeks

  • Delta — how price movement affects your option
  • Theta — time decay, the buyer’s biggest enemy
  • Vega — how volatility changes your premium
  • Gamma — how fast your delta changes
  • How the Greeks combine in a live position

3. Option Pricing & Volatility

  • Implied Volatility (IV) and why it matters
  • IV crush — critical before events
  • Historical vs implied volatility
  • Reading VIX and market sentiment

Who is this course for?

Complete beginners who want to start the right way, and existing buyers who keep losing to time decay and want to understand why.

All teaching is practical and example-driven. This is education only — not tips or profit guarantees. Trading involves real risk, and you always make your own decisions.
