// about me

Hi, I'm Suresh.

Full-Time Options Trader

I have over 15 years of experience in the financial services industry and have been actively involved in the stock market for the last 3 years. Since May 2025, I've been trading full-time in the Futures & Options market.

I started my journey with option buying, where I experienced both profitable and loss-making days. Through continuous learning and market experience, I refined my approach and transitioned to option selling as my primary trading strategy.

Today, I focus on disciplined trading with proper risk management. I believe long-term success in the market comes not from chasing quick profits, but from consistency, patience, and controlling risk.

Through Trade With Suresh, I share market insights, trading knowledge, and my experience to help traders better understand the markets and make informed decisions.
