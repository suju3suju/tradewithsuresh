Options Selling Course

Learn how sellers earn premium, why time decay works in your favour, and how to manage the real risks of selling with disciplined strike and expiry selection.

₹4,999 2 Days IntermediateOnsite & Online

Option selling is how many consistent traders generate income from time decay — but it carries asymmetric risk that must be respected. This course teaches the seller’s mindset: collecting premium, managing margin, reading volatility and choosing strikes wisely.

What you'll learn

1. Foundation

  • What option selling is and how premium is earned
  • Buyer vs seller — risk-reward asymmetry (limited profit, large risk)
  • How time decay works in a seller’s favour
  • Understanding margin — SPAN and Exposure
  • Lot size and realistic capital requirements

2. The Greeks (seller’s view)

  • Theta — the seller’s best friend (time-decay income)
  • Delta — managing directional risk
  • Vega — how rising volatility hurts a seller
  • Gamma — dangerous expiry-day risk
  • How the Greeks combine in a position

3. Volatility — the heart of selling

  • Sell when IV is high, avoid when low
  • IV crush and event-based trades
  • Reading VIX for selling decisions
  • Using IV percentile and IV rank

4. Strike & Expiry Selection

  • Logic behind choosing OTM strikes
  • Delta-based strike selection (0.15–0.30 delta)
  • Weekly vs monthly selling trade-offs
  • Distance from spot — balancing safety and premium

Who is this course for?

Traders who already understand option basics and want a repeatable, risk-managed selling approach.

All teaching is practical and example-driven. This is education only — not tips or profit guarantees. Trading involves real risk, and you always make your own decisions.
